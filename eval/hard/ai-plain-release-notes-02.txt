Release 18.4 of Tallymail ships today to all workspaces. It adds scheduled sends, changes how notifications are grouped and fixes a sync problem on Android.

You can now schedule a message to send at a later time. Write the message as usual, open the menu next to the Send button and choose a date and time. Scheduled messages appear in a new Drafts and Scheduled view, where you can edit or cancel them until the moment they are sent. Messages can be scheduled up to 90 days ahead.

Notifications from the same channel are now grouped into one alert on desktop. If five people reply in a thread within a minute, you will see a single notification with a count instead of five separate ones. You can switch back to the old behaviour in Settings under Notifications. Mentions always arrive as their own alert.

On Android, we fixed an issue where read messages could appear as unread after the app had been closed for more than a day. The fix is included in app version 18.4.1, which is rolling out through Google Play over the next three days.

Workspace admins can also set a default time zone for new members. The setting is in the Admin console under General. It does not change the time zone of existing members.
