Dice & Biscuit is a board-game cafe on Marsh Lane in Leeds. We have a library of 640 games, a kitchen that serves lunch until 3 pm and a bar that closes at 11 pm on weekdays and midnight on Fridays and Saturdays. Entry costs 5 pounds per person, and that covers unlimited play for the whole day.

Games are shelved by weight, from light party games to long strategy titles. Each box carries a coloured label that shows the player count and the usual play time. Our staff have played every game on the shelves and will teach you the rules at your table. A typical teach takes about ten minutes.

The cafe has 22 tables. Four of them are large enough for games with big boards, such as wargames and some cooperative titles. You can book a table online up to 30 days ahead. Walk-ins are welcome, but on Saturday evenings the wait can reach an hour.

We run a beginner night on the first Tuesday of each month and a league night every Thursday. Children are welcome until 7 pm. The cafe is step-free, and we keep a quiet corner for players who prefer less noise. Food is mostly vegetarian, and all dishes are labelled for allergens.
